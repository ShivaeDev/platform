### Breaking

- Modules are named in camelCase or after their export: `with-heavy-lock.ts` becomes `withHeavyLock.ts`, `held-lock.ts` becomes `HeldLock.ts`, `lock-file.ts` becomes `lockFile.ts`, `process-start.ts` becomes `processStart.ts`, and `cli/run-command.ts` becomes `cli/runCommand.ts`.
