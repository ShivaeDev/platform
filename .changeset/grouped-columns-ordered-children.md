### Added

- `@shivaedev/sql`: Models can declare struct-valued groups stored as prefixed flat columns and select or replace a group through repositories.
- `@shivaedev/sql`: Ordered child helpers append, move and reorder under a PostgreSQL parent lock without incrementing sibling versions.
- `@shivaedev/sql`: Versioned repository updates can choose columns while retaining optimistic concurrency checks.
- `@shivaedev/form`: Forms expose typed nested field refs and edits, with a React hook for nested inputs and their path feedback.
