### Added

- An answered Fleet decision that Fleet has not acknowledged yet leaves Needs you for an **Answered, waiting for Fleet** group on Today. Its answer card, Now card and item status say who answered and what, for example "You answered: Request changes. Fleet picks this up on its next pass.", and say that Fleet picks it up once it runs again while Fleet is unavailable. The attention projection carries these rows as `answered`, and inbox rows, work items and document requests carry an `answered` summary.
